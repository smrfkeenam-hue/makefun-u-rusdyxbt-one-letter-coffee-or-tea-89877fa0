# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun's "One Letter: Coffee or Tea" bounty. The entry is the single letter **A** (coffee), chosen because coffee is the livelier, more vivid stance: the letter stands on its own as a whole answer with nothing beside it. `main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) writes exactly that one character, with no trailing newline, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, satisfying the gate's 1-to-1 character requirement.
